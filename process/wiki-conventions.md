# Wiki conventions

- This devwiki/ is the development knowledge base, not the eventual Worldloom world vault.
- Frontmatter wikilinks must be quoted, for example related: ["[[index]]"].
- Keep one principal concept per page where practical.
- Preserve source traceability when migrating material.
- Normative architecture, experimental evidence, vision, process, and reference material retain distinct status boundaries.
- Do not promote proposals or experiments into normative architecture merely by migration.
- Do not design or scaffold the eventual user-facing world-vault schema here.
